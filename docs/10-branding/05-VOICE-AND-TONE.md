# Voice and Tone Guide

## Overview

This document defines how AI Skincare Intelligence System communicates with users across all touchpoints, ensuring consistent, empathetic, and empowering messaging.

---

## Brand Voice

### Core Voice Attributes

Our voice is the consistent personality that comes through in all communications.

| Attribute | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| **Intelligent** | Expert but accessible | "Your skin shows signs of dehydration, which often appears as..." |
| **Empathetic** | Understanding, supportive | "We know skincare can feel overwhelming..." |
| **Empowering** | Builds confidence | "You're making great progress on your skin journey" |
| **Transparent** | Clear, honest | "Here's why we recommend this ingredient..." |
| **Modern** | Contemporary, fresh | "Let's decode your skin's unique needs" |

---

## Tone Variations

While our voice stays consistent, our tone adapts to context.

### By Situation

| Situation | Tone | Example |
|-----------|------|----------|
| **Onboarding** | Warm, welcoming | "Welcome! We're excited to help you understand your skin better." |
| **Analysis Results** | Supportive, informative | "Your analysis reveals some interesting patterns. Let's explore what this means for your routine." |
| **Error/Problem** | Calm, solution-focused | "Something didn't work as expected. Here's how we can fix it together." |
| **Progress** | Celebratory, encouraging | "Amazing progress! Your consistency is really paying off." |
| **Education** | Expert, accessible | "Retinoids work by increasing cell turnover. Here's what that means for you..." |
| **Recommendations** | Confident, helpful | "Based on your profile, we think this could be a great addition to your routine." |

### By User Emotion

| User Feeling | Our Response |
|--------------|---------------|
| Frustrated | Acknowledge, then solve |
| Excited | Match their energy |
| Confused | Simplify, guide step-by-step |
| Skeptical | Provide evidence, be transparent |
| Overwhelmed | Reassure, break down into steps |

## Writing Principles

### 1. Lead with Empathy

**Do:**
> "Dealing with acne can be frustrating. Let's find a routine that works for your unique skin."

**Don't:**
> "Your acne score is high. Follow these steps."

### 2. Explain the 'Why'

**Do:**
> "We recommend niacinamide because it helps regulate oil production and reduces the appearance of pores - both key concerns based on your analysis."

**Don't:**
> "Use niacinamide."

### 3. Be Specific, Not Vague

**Do:**
> "Apply a pea-sized amount to your forehead, cheeks, and chin after cleansing."

**Don't:**
> "Apply the product to your face."

### 4. Celebrate Progress

**Do:**
> "Your hydration levels have improved 23% over the past month. Keep it up!"

**Don't:**
> "Hydration levels: 78%"

### 5. Simplify Complexity

**Do:**
> "Think of your skin barrier as a brick wall - the ceramides are the mortar holding everything together."

**Don't:**
> "Ceramides are sphingolipids that constitute part of the stratum corneum lipid matrix."

## Content Types

### UI Microcopy

| Element | Guidelines | Example |
|---------|------------|----------|
| Button text | Action-oriented, clear | "Start My Analysis", "Get My Routine" |
| Empty states | Helpful, not blaming | "No products saved yet. Let's find some that work for your skin." |
| Loading | Positive, informative | "Analyzing your skin profile..." |
| Success | Celebratory | "All set! Your routine is ready." |
| Error | Solution-focused | "We couldn't process that image. Try with better lighting." |

### Feature Descriptions

**Format:** Benefit first, feature second

**Do:**
> "Understand exactly what your skin needs with AI-powered analysis that examines over 15 skin factors."

**Don't:**
> "Our AI analyzes 15+ skin factors."

### Educational Content

**Structure:**
1. Hook with relevance to user
2. Explain simply
3. Connect to their routine
4. Actionable takeaway

## Language Do's and Don'ts

### Words We Use

| Use | Instead Of |
|-----|------------|
| Personalized | Custom |
| Your skin journey | Treatment |
| Routine | Regimen |
| Understand | Diagnose |
| Analysis | Assessment |
| Recommend | Prescribe |
| Concern | Problem |
| Improve | Fix |

### Phrases We Avoid

| Avoid | Why | Alternative |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| "You must..." | Demanding | "We recommend..." |
| "Obviously..." | Condescending | Remove or explain |
| "Just..." | Dismissive | Be direct |
| "Perfect skin" | Unrealistic | "Your best skin" |
| "Anti-aging" | Negative framing | "Skin health" |
| "Problem areas" | Judgmental | "Areas of focus" |

---

## Inclusive Language

### Principles
- Use gender-neutral language
- Represent diverse skin types and tones
- Avoid assumptions about age, lifestyle, budget
- Be culturally sensitive

### Examples

**Do:**
> "Skincare for every skin type"
> "Your unique skin"

**Don't:**
> "Anti-aging for women over 40"
> "Perfect your complexion"

---

## Grammar & Style

### Capitalization
- **Sentence case** for most UI text
- **Title Case** for headings and navigation
- **Feature names** capitalized (SkinScan, Digital Twin)

### Punctuation
- Use periods in body text
- Omit periods in buttons and headings
- Use exclamation marks sparingly (celebrations only)

### Numbers
- Spell out one through nine
- Use numerals for 10+
- Always use numerals for percentages: "23% improvement"
