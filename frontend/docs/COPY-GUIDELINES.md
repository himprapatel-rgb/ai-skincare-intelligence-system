# Copy Guidelines (Tasks 238, 239, 240, 243)

## Buttons (Task 238)
- **Save** — Saving settings (e.g. Profile, Notification preferences). Use "Save Changes", "Saving...".
- **Submit** — Form submission (e.g. Contact, Register). Use "Submit", "Sign In", "Send Message".
- **Confirm** — Confirm dialogs (destructive or critical). Use "Confirm" / "Cancel" (ConfirmModal).

Context-specific labels are fine: "Create Product", "Update Product", "Get Started", "Start Analysis".

## Links (Task 239)
- **View details** — Navigating to an item/product/detail page (e.g. notification action, product card).
- **Learn more** — Informational (e.g. "Learn more in our Privacy Policy").

## Placeholders (Task 240)
- Prefer **"Enter…"** for text inputs: "Enter your email", "Enter your name", "Enter search term".
- **"Search by…"** is acceptable for search fields: "Search by brand or name".
- Avoid "Type…" in favor of "Enter…".

## Tone (Task 243)
- Friendly and clear; avoid overly formal or clinical language in UI.
- Keep legal and medical disclaimers precise.
- Use "you" and "your"; avoid passive voice where a direct tone is clearer.
