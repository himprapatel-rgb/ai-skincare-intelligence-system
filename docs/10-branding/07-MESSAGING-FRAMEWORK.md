| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "How is this different from other apps?" | Unlike generic apps, we combine AI analysis with dermatological science to give you truly personalized recommendations that evolve with your skin. |
| "Can an app really replace a dermatologist?" | We complement, not replace, professional care. Our AI provides personalized guidance for daily skincare, while flagging concerns that need professional attention. |

| "I've tried personalized skincare before." | True personalization means adapting over time. Our Digital Twin learns from your feedback and results, getting smarter the more you use it. |

## Messaging Don'ts

| Avoid | Why | Alternative |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| "Perfect skin" | Unrealistic, pressure | "Your best skin" |
| "Fix your skin" | Implies broken | "Support your skin" |
| "Anti-aging" | Negative framing | "Skin health", "vitality" |
| "Diagnose" | Medical claim | "Analyze", "understand" |
| "Cure" | Medical claim | "Address", "improve" |
| "Guaranteed results" | Unrealistic | "Personalized approach" |
