## 2. SKIN ANALYSIS PAGE (/scan)

**Status:** Fully Public - Functional without login

**Functionality:**
- Upload Photo or Use Camera options [pellicura](https://pellicura.com/scan)
- Accepts JPG, PNG, WEBP (Max 10MB) [pellicura](https://pellicura.com/scan)
- Face-only requirement: close-up selfie, centered, no group shots [pellicura](https://pellicura.com/scan)

**Best Practices Guidance:**
- Good, even lighting (natural light preferred)
- Remove glasses, face camera directly
- Keep hair away from face
- Photo from shoulders up
- Ensure face is in focus [pellicura](https://pellicura.com/scan)

**Disclaimer:** Clearly states it's for informational use only and not a medical device [pellicura](https://pellicura.com/scan)
