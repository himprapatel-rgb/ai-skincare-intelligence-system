- Primary: #667eea → #764ba2 gradient
- Background: #f8f9fa
- Cards: white with shadows
- Text: #1a1a1a / #64748b

### **Typography:**
- H1: 28px, weight 800
- H2: 22px, weight 700
- H3: 18px, weight 700
- Body: 15-16px

### **Spacing:**
- Card padding: 20px
- Gap: 16px
